What it does

BenchBoss is an Android app for youth football coaches. Add your squad, and it plans substitutions so every player gets a fair share of minutes, then keeps track of who's on, who's next and how long everyone has played during the match. It's free, with no ads and no tracking, and everything you enter stays on your phone.

  • Free
  • No ads
  • No tracking
  • No account
  • Data stays on your phone
