Hazel Cedar

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This privacy policy applies to the "Hazel Cedar" application (the "app"), a personal automation tool operated by Alan Chalmers Consulting (alanchalmersconsulting.com.au).

What the app accesses

The app requests the following Google OAuth scopes:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly — read messages and labels in the authorised Gmail account
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.modify — mark messages as read, apply labels, and archive messages

What the app does with that data

The app is a single-user, personal-use tool. It reads email from the owner's own Gmail account to support automated billing, statement, and notification processing. It does not send email to, read email from, or access the account of any other person.

Hazel Cedar's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Data storage

Email content and metadata processed by the app are stored only on the operator's private, self-hosted server. No email content is sold, shared, transferred to third parties, used for advertising, or used to train any machine learning model. Access tokens and credentials are stored privately on the same server.

Data retention and deletion

Processed data is retained only as long as needed for the owner's records. You can revoke the app's access to your Google account at any time via Google Account permissions, or request deletion of any retained data by contacting the address below.
